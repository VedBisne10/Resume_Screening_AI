JD-015 | NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING (NLP) ENGINEER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Company: LinguaTech AI
Location: Bangalore, India (Hybrid)
Experience: 3–6 Years
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role:
LinguaTech AI is seeking an NLP Engineer to build intelligent language processing
systems that power our conversational AI, document analysis, and information
extraction products.

Responsibilities:
- Design and develop NLP pipelines for text classification, NER, summarization,
  and sentiment analysis.
- Fine-tune pre-trained transformer models (BERT, RoBERTa, T5, GPT) on domain-
  specific datasets.
- Build and evaluate RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines for question
  answering systems.
- Develop data preprocessing and augmentation strategies for NLP tasks.
- Benchmark and compare NLP models using standardized evaluation metrics.
- Integrate NLP models into production APIs and real-time applications.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest NLP research and implement new techniques.

Required Skills:
- Proficiency in Python and deep learning frameworks: PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Hands-on experience with Hugging Face Transformers library and model hub.
- Strong understanding of NLP concepts: tokenization, embeddings, attention mechanisms.
- Experience with text preprocessing tools: SpaCy, NLTK, or Stanza.
- Familiarity with vector stores: Pinecone, Weaviate, FAISS, or ChromaDB.
- Knowledge of evaluation metrics: BLEU, ROUGE, F1-score, Exact Match.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with multilingual NLP models (mBERT, XLM-R).
- Knowledge of speech-to-text systems (Whisper, DeepSpeech).
- Research background or publications in NLP/computational linguistics.
