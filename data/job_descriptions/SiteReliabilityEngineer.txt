JD-019 | SITE RELIABILITY ENGINEER (SRE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Company: Nexus Global Systems
Location: Gurugram, India (Hybrid)
Experience: 4–8 Years
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role:
Nexus Global Systems is seeking a Site Reliability Engineer to ensure the
availability, performance, and scalability of our mission-critical distributed
systems serving millions of users.

Responsibilities:
- Define and enforce SLOs, SLAs, and error budgets for production services.
- Build and maintain observability stacks: metrics, logs, and distributed tracing.
- Automate toil reduction through scripts, tooling, and self-healing systems.
- Lead incident management, root cause analysis, and post-mortem reviews.
- Collaborate with development teams to embed reliability practices into SDLC.
- Design and test disaster recovery and business continuity plans.
- Manage Kubernetes clusters and optimize resource utilization.
- Implement chaos engineering experiments to proactively identify failure modes.

Required Skills:
- Strong background in Linux systems administration and networking.
- Proficiency in programming/scripting: Python, Go, or Bash.
- Deep experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration at scale.
- Expertise in observability tools: Prometheus, Grafana, Jaeger, ELK Stack.
- Experience with cloud platforms: AWS, GCP, or Azure.
- Knowledge of incident management frameworks and on-call practices.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with chaos engineering tools: Chaos Monkey, Gremlin, LitmusChaos.
- Knowledge of eBPF-based observability tools.
- Certifications: CKA, AWS SysOps Administrator, or Google SRE certification.
