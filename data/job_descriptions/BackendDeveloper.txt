JD-006 | BACKEND DEVELOPER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Company: Orbis Cloud Systems
Location: Noida, India (Hybrid)
Experience: 2–5 Years
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role:
Orbis Cloud Systems is seeking a Backend Developer to design, build, and maintain
server-side logic for our SaaS platform serving thousands of enterprise customers.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and maintain high-performance backend services and APIs.
- Design database schemas and write optimized queries.
- Implement business logic, authentication systems, and background jobs.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers to define API contracts.
- Ensure the security, scalability, and reliability of backend systems.
- Write automated tests and maintain CI/CD pipelines.
- Debug and resolve production issues efficiently.
- Contribute to system design discussions and technical documentation.

Required Skills:
- Proficiency in one or more backend languages: Node.js, Python, Go, or Java.
- Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL (MongoDB, Redis).
- Understanding of RESTful and GraphQL API design.
- Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure).
- Knowledge of authentication standards: JWT, OAuth2, SAML.
- Experience with logging, monitoring, and observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana).

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with event-driven architectures and message queues (Kafka, SQS).
- Understanding of distributed systems and CAP theorem.
- Exposure to serverless computing (AWS Lambda, GCP Cloud Functions).
