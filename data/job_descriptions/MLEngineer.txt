JD-002 | MACHINE LEARNING ENGINEER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Company: DataPulse Technologies
Location: Pune, India (On-site)
Experience: 2–5 Years
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role:
DataPulse Technologies is hiring a Machine Learning Engineer to build robust ML
systems that drive business value across our analytics and recommendation products.

Responsibilities:
- Develop end-to-end ML pipelines from data preprocessing to model deployment.
- Work with structured and unstructured data to train classification, regression,
  and clustering models.
- Optimize models for performance, scalability, and cost-efficiency.
- Implement feature engineering strategies and maintain feature stores.
- Collaborate with data engineers to build reliable data pipelines.
- Automate model retraining and monitoring using CI/CD for ML.
- Write clean, testable, and production-ready code.

Required Skills:
- Proficiency in Python, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, or CatBoost.
- Experience with deep learning frameworks: Keras, PyTorch, or TensorFlow.
- Solid understanding of statistics, probability, and linear algebra.
- Familiarity with data processing tools: Pandas, NumPy, PySpark.
- Experience with model deployment using FastAPI, Flask, or Docker.
- Knowledge of experiment tracking with MLflow or similar tools.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with time series forecasting models (ARIMA, Prophet, LSTM).
- Hands-on with Kubernetes-based ML deployments.
- B.Tech/M.Tech in Computer Science, Statistics, or related discipline.
