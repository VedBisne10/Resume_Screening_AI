JD-013 | CYBERSECURITY ENGINEER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Company: SecureShield Technologies
Location: Delhi NCR, India (On-site)
Experience: 3–7 Years
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role:
SecureShield Technologies is hiring a Cybersecurity Engineer to protect our
digital assets, identify vulnerabilities, and build a security-first culture
across the organization.

Responsibilities:
- Conduct penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and security audits.
- Monitor and respond to security incidents using SIEM tools (Splunk, QRadar).
- Implement and manage endpoint detection and response (EDR) solutions.
- Design and enforce network security policies (firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPN).
- Perform threat modeling and risk assessments for applications and infrastructure.
- Develop and maintain security playbooks and incident response procedures.
- Ensure compliance with security frameworks: ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST, PCI-DSS.
- Educate internal teams on security best practices and phishing awareness.

Required Skills:
- Expertise in network security: firewalls, VPNs, WAF, IDS/IPS.
- Hands-on experience with penetration testing tools: Metasploit, Burp Suite, Nmap.
- Knowledge of OWASP Top 10 and common vulnerability exploitation techniques.
- Proficiency with SIEM platforms: Splunk, QRadar, or Microsoft Sentinel.
- Understanding of cryptography, PKI, and SSL/TLS protocols.
- Familiarity with cloud security in AWS, Azure, or GCP.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Certifications: CEH, CISSP, OSCP, or CompTIA Security+.
- Experience with threat intelligence platforms and dark web monitoring.
- Knowledge of Zero Trust architecture.
